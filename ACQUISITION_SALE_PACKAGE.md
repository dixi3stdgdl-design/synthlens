## 1. Executive Summary

**SynthLens** is the world’s first AI-powered acoustic signature detection engine and hardware synthesizer laboratory. Designed for sound designers, music producers, vintage instrument retailers, and audio engineers, SynthLens analyzes incoming audio in real-time, extracts 18 fundamental acoustic features (formants, harmonic decay, THD, spectral rolloff, crest factor), and instantly identifies the exact hardware synthesizer model, filter topology, and oscillator architecture.

The project is packaged as a **Turnkey Commercial Asset**, ready for immediate publication to Google Play, App Store, and enterprise deployment.
